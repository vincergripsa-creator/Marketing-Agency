# Paid ad creatives that sell low-ticket digital products (PDF guides, templates, planners, mini-courses) on TikTok, Meta, YouTube Shorts and Pinterest, 2024-2026

*Legend for every claim: **[M-PLAT]** measured by the platform that sells the ad space (method often opaque, self-interested). **[M-IND]** measured on an independent or third-party dataset. **[M-ACAD]** academic paper or preprint. **[GUIDE]** official platform guidance with no data. **[CLAIM]** agency, vendor or creator claim, not verified. **[OLD yyyy]** data from before 2024. "Hit rate" (Motion) = the share of a format's creatives that become "winners", meaning they spend at least 10x the account's median ad. It measures how Meta's delivery allocates spend, not ROAS or CPA. Research done 2026-11-05. Motion's research pages now redirect (301) to runneth.com, and the figures there are still Motion's "Creative Benchmarks 2026".*

## 1. Formats: which formats perform for digital products, by platform, with which metrics

### Takeaway
No public dataset isolates low-ticket digital products. The largest independent creative-level dataset (Motion: 550,000+ Meta ads, about $1.3B in spend, Sep 2025 to Jan 2026, including Black Friday) finds that text-forward statics produce winners most often: text-only 11.6%, product image with text 8.75%. UGC follows at 7.56%. The strongest video formats are unboxing (9.8%), offer-first banner (8.7%), behind the scenes (8.6%), founder (8.6%), POV (8.3%) and demo (8.1%). Screen recording (5.5%), listicle (5.3%), review (4.9%), green screen (4.9%), animation (4.6%) and carousel (4.45%) trail. Platform tests point the same way: 9:16 video with sound costs 34.5% less per result than images on Reels. TikTok (data from 2021) and Shorts reward vertical framing, 720p or better, voiceover or music, an on-screen text CTA and several scenes.

### Inferences
- **Translating Motion's formats to a PDF product:** "Unboxing" (9.83%) becomes a "what's inside" reveal or flip-through. "Demo" (8.11%) becomes the template or planner being filled in or used. "Offer-first banner" (8.68%) becomes a price anchor or bundle value ("40 templates, $9"). "Letter", "sign" and "unconventional text placement" (7.9 to 10.8%) become notes-app, sticky-note or handwritten text statics. Raw "screen recording" scores lower (5.49%) than "demo" (8.11%). This suggests showing the product in use, with context and a person's voice or hands, beats a bare screen capture.
- Text-only and product-with-text statics are cheap, so their lead may partly reflect volume plus holiday offers. An automated designer should treat them as the high-volume testing layer and judge winners on CPA, not on hit rate alone.
- At low ticket ($5 to $50) there is little margin for expensive production. The evidence favours cheap, high-volume formats (text statics, mockup statics, faceless voiceover, UGC). High production adds little to hit rate (6.97%).
- By platform. **Meta:** text statics, mockup with text, offer-first, and demo/unboxing-style video plus creator variants. **TikTok:** 9:16 video of 21 to 34 s with voiceover, a text CTA and several scenes, plus photo carousels of 3 or 7 to 9 slides with music. **Shorts:** vertical, with sound, 10 to 30 s for conversion objectives. **Pinterest:** 2:3 static mockups with a text overlay at the top.

#### Distinct creative styles to give automated designers (evidence anchor in brackets)
1. **Text-only "note" static**: one bold line (confession or claim) on a plain, notes-app, sticky-note or handwritten-letter background. [Motion: text only 11.60%, letter 10.83%, unconventional text placement 9.63%, sign 7.86%]
2. **Mockup + benefit headline static**: 3D cover or tablet/phone mockup showing interior pages, with a headline and a sub-benefit. [product image with text 8.75%]
3. **Offer-first banner**: price anchor or bundle value plus a deadline. [offer-first banner 8.68%; price-anchor hook 10.89%, inflated by Black Friday]
4. **"What's inside" flip-through**: fast page-by-page reveal by hands flipping a printed copy or scrolling a tablet, with optional page-turn sounds. [unboxing 9.83%, ASMR 8.58%, demo 8.11%]
5. **Demo in use**: the template or planner being filled with real data, framed in a device mockup, with voiceover and captions. [demo 8.11% vs raw screen recording 5.49%; TikTok voiceover +12% conversion rate (unverified)]
6. **Faceless voiceover + b-roll, problem to solution**: a 21 to 34 s TikTok cut and a 10 to 30 s Shorts cut. [cinematic b-roll 6.85%; TikTok length +280%, voiceover + written offer +87% (2021); Shorts sound +20%]
7. **Creator UGC review / talking head**, run as a partnership or Spark ad. [UGC 7.56%, influencer endorsement 7.71%; Meta partnership-ad lift studies; creator-led ads +39% attention]
8. **Founder / behind the scenes**: "why I made this planner" or "how I built this guide". [founder 8.57%, behind the scenes 8.64%]
9. **POV / meme text overlay**: "POV: you finally have a meal plan". [POV 8.28%; meme ranks in Motion's Fashion top 10]
10. **TikTok photo carousel**: hook slide, a continuous story, CTA slide last, music, 3 or 7 to 9 slides. [TikTok carousel playbook; Meta carousel only 4.45%, on a very small sample]
11. **Listicle / feature-benefit**: "5 pages that saved my budget". [listicle 5.30%, feature-benefit 5.61%: lower, keep as a minority of tests]
12. **Testimonial / review montage**: screenshots of buyer reviews over the product. [testimonial 6.57%, review 4.89%, montage 7.02%]
13. **Before/after, us vs them, split screen**: messy spreadsheet vs clean template, or "without vs with the planner". [before & after 6.07%, us vs them 6.52%, split screen 5.62%; avoid body-image before/after under Meta policy, see section 3]
14. **Stitch / reaction**: a creator reacting to a pain-point post, then showing the guide. [stitch 12.50% and reaction 11.24% in the vertical that appears to be Health & Wellness]
15. **Green screen**: creator in front of the sales page. [green screen 4.87%, low priority]
16. **Pinterest 2:3 seasonal mockup pin**: headline overlay at the top, product in the middle, details and logo at the bottom. [Pinterest guidance]

### Gaps
- No platform or independent source publishes creative data for a "digital products", "info products" or "courses" vertical. Motion's "Education" vertical data is only in the PDF (pp. 15 to 20), which could not be opened.
- No hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPA or ROAS **by format** was found for any platform. Motion reports hit rate only, Meta cost per result, TikTok conversion lift, Google conversions per dollar.
- No independent creative-level dataset for TikTok, Shorts or Pinterest was found. Motion's data is Meta only.
- Organic TikTok photo-mode performance is contested. Search extracts cite a 2025 analysis of about 698k posts with higher engagement for carousels, and a 2026 Metricool study of 2.3M posts in which image posts got about a fifth of the views of videos. Neither primary source was opened.
- No Varos or Foreplay creative-format data was retrieved. A Triple Whale "Books & Music" cost-per-purchase figure ($30.69, ROAS 1.65) appeared in a search extract but could not be traced to its page.

## 2. Hooks: the first 1-3 seconds, text overlays, captions and safe zones

### Takeaway
The only large measured comparison of hook types (Motion, Meta, holiday window) shows promotional hooks win most often: newness 11.4%, price anchor 10.9%, urgency 9.7%, offer only 9.3%, FOMO 9.2%. Confession (8.7%), curiosity (7.8%) and bold claim (7.2%) come next. Question (5.5%), how-to (5.5%), listicle (5.5%) and explainer (5.2%) openers trail. A preprint on 46,605 organic TikTok hooks also favours curiosity gaps and urgency over direct address and instructions. Platform evidence says the product or brand, the message and sound should appear in the first 2 to 3 s, with on-screen text and a CTA, inside the safe zones (Reels: about 14% top, 35% bottom, 6% sides).

### Cited Findings

**Measured hook effects**
- [M-IND] Motion hook tactics (Meta, same 2025-26 dataset; one tactic label per creative combining hook and headline): newness 11.37% (83 winners), sale announcement 11.35% (16 winners), price anchor 10.89% (71), urgency 9.73% (4,855), offer only 9.29% (4,798), FOMO 9.20% (364), confession 8.74% (675), curiosity 7.77% (2,402), bold claim 7.19% (2,308), relatability 6.85% (3,377), contrast 6.78% (2,035), authority 6.40% (590). Also: storytelling 6.23%, question 5.47%, how-to 5.47%, listicle 5.45%, explainer 5.24%. — [Runneth/Motion](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026)
- [M-IND] Motion's reading: the promotional cluster (newness, sale, price anchor, urgency, offer only, FOMO) is probably inflated by Black Friday. The "interrupt" cluster (confession, contrarian, shocking statement, warning, reverse psychology, myth busting, bold claim) has lower hit rates and higher variance, but holds steadier across seasons. Top tactics land at about 6 to 11% against a 4 to 8% tier average. Rankings are not ROAS-weighted. Motion advises keeping both clusters in the test mix. — [Runneth/Motion, top hook tactics](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-hook-tactics)
- [M-ACAD, preprint, organic posts] 46,605 TikTok hooks (the opening line of text or speech) across 20+ categories. Curiosity gaps and urgency beat direct address and instructional phrasing in most categories. Emotional hooks (about 3.2% of posts) earn proportionally more comments and shares. The best hook length varies by niche, from about 9 words (news) to about 90 (entertainment). Not peer-reviewed (OSF, Aug 2025), single author. — [Mishra 2025 via Sciety](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.31235/osf.io/rj2mz_v1)
- [M-ACAD] One e-commerce company's TikTok ad data: viewer churn rises sharply at the start of exposure, with more than 80% of viewers typically lost early in the video (ACM Hypertext 2024). The full text was blocked (403), so this is from the abstract or extract. — [Salminen et al. 2024](https://dl.acm.org/doi/fullHtml/10.1145/3648188.3677048)
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2016] Facebook/Nielsen: up to 47% of a video campaign's value comes in the first 3 s, and up to 74% within 10 s. — [Fox Business](https://foxbusiness.com/markets/have-a-second-that-may-be-all-facebooks-video-ads-will-need). Elsewhere framed as lift: 3 s of viewing raises ad recall 47% and awareness 32%, and 10 s raises them 74% and 65%. Also, 65% of people who watch 3 s reach 10 s, and 45% reach 30 s. — [Salesforce blog, Feb 2016](https://salesforce.com/content/blogs/us/en/2016/02/facebook-video-ads-global.html)
- [M-PLAT + IND] A sonic brand asset in the first 2 seconds gives +191% brand-awareness lift. Strong early branding cuts negative sentiment 48% and attention decay 19% (System1 x TikTok, 887 ads). — [Chief Marketer](https://www.chiefmarketer.com/system1-tiktok-study-branding-elements-and-creator-led-content-improve-short-form-video-ads/)
- [M-IND, cited by Meta] CreativeX: about 45% of creator ad spend is wasted when the brand is not shown in the first 3 seconds (cited in coverage of Meta's Oct 2026 creator white paper). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-says-partnership-ads-cut-incremental-purchase-costs-45-in-emea-tests/)
- [GUIDE][OLD 2021 data] Google ABCD (Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction). Open with an immersive story, get to the point fast, use tight framing and bright, high-contrast visuals, and back the message with audio and text. For Shorts, Google's line is "Announce yourself with audio". Google/Kantar (Apr 2021, method and sample not stated) link ABCD to +30% short-term sales likelihood and +17% long-term brand contribution. — [Google Ads Help, ABCDs](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en); [Think with Google](https://business.google.com/us/think/future-of-marketing/youtube-video-ad-creative/)
- [GUIDE] TikTok hook templates: popularity ("sold out 3x, back in stock"), tip or hack for a problem, price or promo, calling out the audience ("Are you a homeowner?"), and listing selling points ("3 reasons why..."). TikTok adds that every hook needs a payoff. — [TikTok SMB Creative Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf)

**Hook metrics and benchmarks (vendor heuristics)**
- [CLAIM] Meta hook rate = 3-second plays ÷ impressions. TikTok versions often use 2-second views. — [Sepia Lab](https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/hook-rate-benchmarks)
- [CLAIM] Thumbstop on cold prospecting runs about 18 to 28% (median about 22%), against 30 to 45% (median about 36%) on warm retargeting. — [AdSights](https://www.adsights.ai/resources/glossary/metrics/thumbstop-rate-tsr)
- [CLAIM] Blogs credit Motion (2025) with a "solid" hook rate of about 25 to 35% and an "average" hold rate of 40 to 50%. Motion's 2026 benchmark pages publish no hook, thumbstop or hold rate, so that attribution is unverified. — [BiddyCo](https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/hook-rate-benchmarks-meta); [Runneth/Motion](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026)

**Text overlays, captions, CTA**
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2021] Text CTA +152% conversion; captions or on-screen offer/CTA +80% (e-commerce); text within the first 7 s +43% (gaming). — [TikTok For Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions)
- [GUIDE] TikTok carousels: keep text concise and easy to read, carrying the core message, price or key feature. Avoid white, vague, too-big or too-small text. Put the CTA after the benefits, and keep colours, fonts and logos consistent. — [TikTok Image Ads Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Image_Ads_Carousel_Ads_Playbook.pdf)
- [GUIDE] Pinterest: key message as a text overlay at the top, visual in the middle, extra details at the bottom. Titles take up to 100 characters, and the first 40 are most likely to show. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)
- [GUIDE] Shorts: the CTA button appears 3 s in for Performance Max, App and Demand Gen, and 10 s in for Video view and Video reach. The CTA overlay card takes a 40-character headline and a 90-character description. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en)

**Safe zones**
- [GUIDE] Meta: keep key messages and visuals clear of the Reels interface at the edges and bottom. Meta provides safe-zone templates (.ppt/.psd/.keynote) and a Design Check app. — [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- [CLAIM citing Meta specs] Reels ads: leave about 14% at the top, 35% at the bottom and 6% on each side free of text and logos (about 269/672/65 px on 1080x1920). Keep about 40% at the bottom clear if the ad carries a disclaimer. — [Adnova](https://adnova.ai/blogs/meta-ad-safe-zones-guide); [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/instagram-reels-safe-zone)
- [CLAIM] TikTok in-feed on 1080x1920: keep clear about 240 px at the top, 660 px at the bottom and 120 px on each side. The action buttons also cover a column about 180 px wide on the right, from about 840 px down. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/tools/safe-zones/tiktok)

**Sound**
- [GUIDE / M-PLAT] Reels ads autoplay with sound unless the user has muted. Meta's 34.5% result bundles sound with 9:16. — [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads). Shorts: sound gives "over 20%" more conversions. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en). TikTok: always include sound, and music is mandatory for image and carousel ads. — [TikTok SMB Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf); [TikTok Image Ads Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Image_Ads_Carousel_Ads_Playbook.pdf)

### Inferences
#### Hook rules for automated designers
1. Every video carries a **frame-1 text hook**: a single idea, high contrast, placed in the upper-middle band, below the top 14% and above the bottom 35% keep-out zone. On TikTok, also keep it off the right-hand button column.
2. Show the **product or brand cue within 2 to 3 s**: a mockup, a page, or the guide's name said aloud. This rests on System1, CreativeX and ABCD. Creator footage must not run 3 s without the product.
3. **Rotate hook families on purpose.** About 40% promotional (price anchor, offer only, urgency, newness/"just dropped"), about 40% interrupt (confession, curiosity gap, bold claim, contrarian, warning), about 20% other (audience call-out, relatability, how-to). Treat plain questions and generic explainers as low priority on Meta. On promotional hooks, discount the Black Friday inflation outside Q4.
4. **Make hooks specific** (number, audience, outcome), because the preprint and the vendors agree that generic openers underperform. Digital-product examples: "Teachers: steal my 2026 lesson-plan template", "I wasted 3 years before making this budget sheet" (confession), "This 12-page guide replaced a $300 course" (bold claim), "Normally $49, $9 this week" (price anchor), "The page that finally fixed my mornings" (curiosity).
5. Put a **text CTA** on screen and say it in voiceover at the end. On Shorts, keep CTA copy within 40/90 characters.
6. **Track** hook rate (3 s on Meta, 2 s on TikTok), hold rate, CTR and CPA per creative. Use vendor bands only as soft kill thresholds: for example, cold Meta hook rate well under about 20% suggests the opening frame is failing.

### Gaps
- No controlled ad test that isolates hook type (question vs claim vs curiosity) with CPA as the outcome was found. Motion is observational, spend-based and holiday-window. The preprint covers organic posts and engagement.
- The widely repeated claim that "63% of TikTok's highest-CTR ads show the key message in the first 3 seconds" could not be traced to a primary TikTok document.
- No platform publishes a minimum font size. Third-party guides disagree (about 32 vs 40 pt).
- The "85% of Facebook video is watched without sound" figure that circulates is old and not from Meta. No current sound-off share for Reels, TikTok or Shorts was found.
- TikTok's official safe-zone pixel values were not retrieved directly; only third-party templates were.

## 3. Visual patterns: product early vs late, face vs no face, first-frame motion, colour contrast, native vs polished, creator-made vs brand-made

### Takeaway
The evidence favours showing the product or brand early (within 2 to 3 s), a native but technically sharp look (720p+, 9:16), several scenes and pacing, and bright, high-contrast frames. Creator-made ads are measurably more efficient in Meta's own studies: 45% lower median cost per incremental purchase across 20 EMEA lift studies when at least 30% of assets are partnership ads. Faces are a lever, not a requirement: faceless text-forward statics have the highest hit rates on Meta.

### Cited Findings

**Product and brand early vs late**
- [GUIDE] ABCD: introduce the brand or product early and keep it present; spoken brand mentions strengthen on-screen branding. For action objectives, "make the product the ad" and ask after the context is set. For full-funnel ads, start with branding and end with the product. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en)
- [M-PLAT + IND] System1 x TikTok: strong vs weak early branding means 48% less negative sentiment, 57% more happiness and 19% less attention decay. Logos placed in context gave +182% awareness lift, while logo overlays reduced it. Creator-led ads with strong early branding gave 2.3x awareness lift and 3x brand-image lift. — [Chief Marketer](https://www.chiefmarketer.com/system1-tiktok-study-branding-elements-and-creator-led-content-improve-short-form-video-ads/)
- [M-IND, cited by Meta] About 45% of creator spend is wasted without the brand in the first 3 s (CreativeX). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-says-partnership-ads-cut-incremental-purchase-costs-45-in-emea-tests/)
- [GUIDE] Pinterest: make the product the focus in realistic settings, avoid abstract or lifestyle-only images, and show the logo on every ad without letting it dominate. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)

**Face vs no face**
- [M-ACAD][OLD 2014, organic] About 1 million Instagram photos: those with faces were 38% more likely to get likes and 32% more likely to get comments (Bakhshi, Shamma & Gilbert, CHI 2014). — [Georgia Tech News](https://www.news.gatech.edu/news/2014/03/20/face-it-instagram-pictures-faces-are-more-popular)
- [M-IND] Meta ads: faceless text-only (11.60%) and product with text (8.75%) beat founder (8.57%), influencer endorsement (7.71%) and UGC (7.56%) on hit rate. Founder, POV and behind the scenes are still among the top video formats. — [Runneth/Motion](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026)
- [GUIDE] Google: feature people. For Shorts, let talent look authentic and show them using the product. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en). TikTok: feature creators, employees and customers. In carousels, show scenario-based benefits with a person dealing with the pain point. — [TikTok SMB Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf); [TikTok Image Ads Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Image_Ads_Carousel_Ads_Playbook.pdf)

**Creator-made vs brand-made**
- [M-PLAT] Meta white paper (7 Oct 2026; meta-analysis of 20 EMEA conversion-lift studies, Jan to Jun 2025). Business-as-usual campaigns compared with ones where at least 30% of assets were partnership ads: the latter had 45% lower median cost per incremental purchase and 35% higher median incremental ROAS, at 92% confidence (below the usual 95%). It also cites, from older data: 19% lower acquisition cost (15 tests, 2021-22) [OLD]; +71% brand intent when partnership ads exceed 20% of spend (14 brand-lift studies, 2023-24) [OLD]. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-says-partnership-ads-cut-incremental-purchase-costs-45-in-emea-tests/)
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2022-23] Creator content on Reels: 5% lower cost per result, 11% higher conversion rate. — [Meta for Business](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- [CLAIM, vendor with a stake] Agentio, which sells creator campaigns, analysed $130M of Meta spend: partnership ads had 19% higher CTR, 10% higher conversion rate and 5% lower CPA. — [Noovid](https://noovid.com/en/blog/meta-partnership-ads-vs-brand-ads)
- [M-PLAT + IND] Creator-led ads capture 39% more attention than traditional ads on TikTok. — [Chief Marketer](https://www.chiefmarketer.com/system1-tiktok-study-branding-elements-and-creator-led-content-improve-short-form-video-ads/)
- [GUIDE] Shorts: use authentic creator content, and use Brand Partner Access to boost creators' organic Shorts. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en). TikTok Creative Challenge: up to 30 creator-made ads within 10 days. — [TikTok SMB Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf)

**Native vs polished**
- [M-IND] High production 6.97% against UGC 7.56% and text-only 11.60%: mid-table. Motion notes the "high production" label is coarse. — [Runneth/Motion](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026); [top asset types](https://runneth.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-asset-types)
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2021] 720p or better: +312% conversion; 9:16 instead of black bars: +91%. — [TikTok For Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions)
- [GUIDE] TikTok: smartphone lo-fi is fine, but no pixelated or blurry video and nothing below 720p. — [TikTok SMB Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf). Google: make Shorts ads feel organic and blend in, built "social first". — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en)

**Motion, pacing, colour**
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2021] Several scenes +38% (e-commerce, against one continuous shot of one person); 5+ scenes +171% (gaming). — [TikTok For Business](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions)
- [M-ACAD] TikTok ads: colloquial expression, cadence, colourfulness and visual prominence correlate significantly with engagement (Journal of Business Research 2025, multiple regression; read at abstract level). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296325004850)
- [M-ACAD] Among 110 viral marketer-made TikTok videos, those mixing creative and informative content spread more often. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369617175_Short_Vertical_Videos_Going_Viral_on_TikTok_An_Empirical_Study_and_Sentiment_Analysis)
- [GUIDE] Google: engaging pacing, tight framing, bright and high-contrast visuals, nothing competing for attention. — [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en). Pinterest: use "bits of motion" to stand out. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)

**Policy constraint on before/after**
- [GUIDE] Meta Health & Wellness: ads must not imply a negative body self-image. Cosmetics and digital editing apps may show before/after without negative self-perception. Health and weight-loss ads must target 18+. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness). Third-party guides list before/after visuals among common Pinterest ad rejections. — [Cropink](https://cropink.com/pinterest-advertising-guidelines)

### Inferences
#### Visual rules for automated designers
1. **Frame 1** shows the product (cover or interior-page mockup, a hand holding the printout, or the device screen) plus the hook text. For creator videos, the product appears by second 2 to 3.
2. **Native but sharp:** export 1080x1920 at 720p minimum, phone-shot look, no black bars, no landscape crops.
3. **Pacing:** in videos, aim for at least 3 to 5 distinct scenes or cuts in 15 to 34 s and avoid one long take. This extrapolates TikTok's 2021 multi-scene lifts, which were measured on e-commerce and gaming.
4. **Contrast:** bright backgrounds, text in high-contrast boxes, one accent colour per creative. Vary the palette across variants so creatives do not look alike. No measured palette data was found.
5. **Faces as an A/B axis, not a default:** run the same concept as a faceless version (hands, voiceover, mockup) and a face version (creator or founder). Low-ticket economics favour the faceless one unless face versions win on CPA.
6. **Creator variants:** for scaling on Meta, allocate a share of concepts (Meta's tests used at least 30% of assets) to creator or partnership versions. Their branding must be in context and early, not a logo slapped on.
7. **Before/after for productivity, budget or organisation guides** (messy vs organised) is allowed and useful. For fitness or diet guides, avoid body before/after and any negative self-image framing.

### Gaps
- No measured face vs faceless test for digital-product ads was found. The face evidence is organic (2014) or about engagement only.
- No platform quantifies "motion in the first frame" or first-frame movement separately from hook text.
- No measured data on specific colours or contrast ratios in ads was found. Google gives only guidance; the 2025 paper measures "colourfulness" against engagement, not sales.
- Teixeira-style academic work on early branding and ad avoidance (from TV and online video) was not retrieved in this session.

## 4. Creative fatigue: how fast creatives wear out, how many variants advertisers run, refresh cadence

### Takeaway
No platform publishes a fixed lifespan. The cleanest measured curve is old: AdEspresso (2018, 500 Meta campaigns) found CTR down 23% at frequency 4, 30% at 5 and 41% at 6, while CPC rose 68%, 99% and 127%. TikTok's own materials range from refreshing about weekly (older) to every 2 to 4 weeks (newer), with 3 to 5 (or 5 to 7) creatives per ad group and at least 4 new creatives per campaign per month. Motion's data shows Meta accounts launching 2.8 to 18.9 new creatives a week by spend tier, with only about 4 to 8% becoming winners. That is a volume game, and conceptual diversity matters more than tweaks.

### Cited Findings
- [M-IND][OLD 2018] AdEspresso (500 campaigns), CTR and CPC change by frequency: f2: CTR −8.9%, CPC +49.8%; f3: −16.9%, +62.2%; f4: −23.3%, +68.0%; f5: −29.7%, +98.5%; f6: −41.2%, +127.3%; f7: −41.4%, +127.3%; f8: −49.0%, +138.3%; f9: −49.9%, +161.2%. AdEspresso acts when frequency nears 5 and says never exceed 10. — [AdEspresso](https://adespresso.com/academy/blog/facebook-ads-frequency/)
- [M-IND, date unclear] Merkle analysis: CPC started to suffer seriously from ad fatigue after about 35 days. — [MarTech](https://martech.org/ad-fatigue-social-media-combat)
- [GUIDE, TikTok, via search extracts; Creative Center pages returned HTTP 500] TikTok's guidance varies by document:
  - Older creative guidance and a best-practices playbook: refresh about every 7 days, or as performance requires. — [TikTok Creative Guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/tiktok_creative_accelerator/pc/en); [TikTok Best Practices Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Follow_Me_Best_Practices_Playbook_EN.pdf)
  - Lead-generation guide: fatigue sets in about 5 to 7 days into a run; use 3 to 5 creatives per ad group. — [TikTok Lead Gen guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_LeadGeneration_AgencySMB.pdf)
  - Newer creative guide: refresh when CTR or engagement drops; many brands refresh every 2 to 4 weeks. — [TikTok creative advertising guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/what-is-ad-creative-guide)
  - Return on Influence page: 5 to 7 varied creatives plus weekly refreshes. — [TikTok Return on Influence](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/return-on-influence-tiktok-creative-impact/pc/en)
  - Performance Fundamentals 2024: at least 4 new creatives per campaign per month. — [TikTok Performance Fundamentals 2024](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Performance_Fundamentals_2024.pdf)
  - Smart Creative: detects fatigue and rotates assets automatically. — [TikTok Smart Creative help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/smart-creative?lang=en)
  - Search Ads are described as far less prone to fatigue. — [TikTok Search Ads creative help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/search-ads-creative)
- [GUIDE] The TikTok SMB playbook defines creative fatigue as an audience seeing the same ad too often and engaging less. It describes Smart Creative as auto-generating combinations, detecting fatigue early and auto-refreshing assets. — [TikTok SMB Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/SMB_Creative_Playbook_External.pdf)
- [M-PLAT] Over 60% of TikTok advertisers see a positive correlation between number of creatives and campaign effectiveness. — [TikTok Image Ads Playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Image_Ads_Carousel_Ads_Playbook.pdf)
- [M-IND, via a secondary summary] Motion: average new creatives per week by monthly spend tier, top quartile in brackets. Under $10K: 2.80 (4.83). $10-50K: 4.10 (8.09). $50-200K: 6.67 (15.95). $200K-1M: 11.24 (31.11). Over $1M: 18.85 (54.64). Top-quartile accounts launch about 2 to 3x more than their peers. The values come from the secondary summary because Motion's own page shows placeholders. — [Sepia Lab summary of Motion](https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/ad-creative-volume-benchmarks)
- [M-IND] Winner rate is about 5% overall, from about 3.8% (micro accounts) to about 8.2% (enterprise). Larger accounts find more winners mainly because they test more. — [Motion, 10x benchmark (search extract)](https://motionapp.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/10x-benchmark); [Runneth/Motion](https://runneth.com/thumbstop-pulse/creative-benchmarks-2026)
- [CLAIM] Meta publishes no ads-per-ad-set quota after its Andromeda retrieval update. Third parties say Meta now calls creative diversification the main lever and groups near-duplicate ads together. Their recommendations range from 4 to 6 up to 15 to 25 distinct creatives per ad set. — [Daumenstopp](https://daumenstopp.de/en/guides/meta-creatives-andromeda.html); [UGC Builder](https://ugcbuilder.com/blog/meta-andromeda)
- [CLAIM, vendor data] AdScale (1,200+ e-commerce accounts): ad sets with 4 to 6 active creatives performed best. — [AdScale](https://adscale.com/blog/how-many-ad-creatives-per-ad-set/)
- [CLAIM] Claimed lifespans: TikTok about 3 to 7 days vs Meta about 14 to 21. For app ads: 5 to 14 days on TikTok vs 3 to 6 weeks on Meta. One blog says TikTok research puts consumer-campaign saturation at 7 to 10 days. None of this is primary. — [Influee](https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-ad-fatigue); [Riffkit](https://riffkit.ai/blog/tiktok-creative-fatigue); [Viralway](https://www.viralway.co/blog/creative-fatigue-app-ads)
- [CLAIM] A CTR drop of 25 to 35% from the launch baseline is a clear fatigue signal (vendor heuristic). — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/ad-creative-fatigue-guide.md)
- [M-ACAD] A WPP preprint (2026) proposes a fatigue-detection method tested on 264 synthetic daily trajectories. The author says it is not causal fatigue attribution. — [arXiv 2509.09758](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.09758)
- [GUIDE] Pinterest: monitor engagement to decide when to refresh, and refresh for seasonal moments. No fixed schedule. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)

### Inferences
- **Cadence for a small digital-product advertiser (under $10K a month):** about 3 to 5 genuinely new concepts a week to match Motion's top quartile in that tier. Expect about 1 winner per 20 to 25 launches. Each concept should differ in hook family plus format, not just colour or font, because Meta groups near-duplicates.
- **Refresh triggers to give the system:** 7-day frequency of 3 to 4 or more on prospecting (AdEspresso shows CTR −17% to −23% at f3 to f4); CTR down about 25 to 35% from the creative's first-3-day baseline; or CPA rising above target for 3 or more days. On TikTok, review weekly and expect to replace most of the set every 1 to 4 weeks.
- **Iterating on winners:** keep the winning concept and swap hooks and first frames. The hook-tactic and format lists in sections 1 and 2 are the variation axes.
- These triggers are inferences. The only numeric decay curve found is from 2018, on Meta.

### Gaps
- No primary, current, platform-published frequency-to-CPA decay curve exists for TikTok, Reels, Shorts or Pinterest.
- Meta's in-product "creative fatigue" diagnostic definition was not verified on Meta's help centre. Third parties describe it as cost per result well above the advertiser's past ads.
- A Motion statistic that about 50 to 53% of ads are switched off before 28 days circulates in secondary blogs and could not be verified.
- No Pinterest-specific fatigue or creative-lifespan data was found.

## 5. Pinterest specifics for digital products: pin formats, text overlay, Idea Pins, seasonality

### Takeaway
Pinterest publishes guidance, not performance data. That guidance is a 2:3 (1000x1500) image with the product as hero, a concise text overlay at the top, the logo on every ad and a specific CTA, plus "bits of motion". Idea Pins no longer exist as a separate format. Their features (music, stickers, text, product tags) now belong to all Pins. The best-documented lever is timing. Pinterest says festive searches start as early as July, Halloween searches grow from August, and holiday planning has shifted earlier. Seasonal printables and planners should therefore be live months ahead.

### Cited Findings
- [GUIDE] Standard image ad: 2:3 at 1000x1500 (larger ratios may be cut off in feeds), PNG/JPEG, maximum 20 MB (desktop) or 32 MB (app). Title up to 100 characters, with the first 40 most likely to show. Description up to 800 characters. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)
- [GUIDE] Layout and content: visual in the middle, key message as a text overlay at the top, details at the bottom. Logo or brand name on every ad, visible but not dominant. Concise messaging and a specific CTA on each asset. Product as the focus in realistic settings, avoiding abstract or lifestyle-only images. Diverse, relatable people. Motion to stand out. Test with A/B or multi-objective tests. Use Pinterest Predicts and the Pinterest Palette for trend ideas. — [Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)
- [GUIDE] Idea Pins were folded into standard Pins, and their features (music, product tagging, stickers) are available for all video and image Pins. Existing multi-page Pins now display as videos, but carousel Pins can still be created. A Pinterest staff reply says Idea Pins are no longer available. — [Pinterest Business Community FAQ](https://community.pinterest.biz/topic/59473); [community thread](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/idea-pins-vs-pins-question/42594)
- [M-PLAT][OLD 2020] Pinners usually start holiday planning in September. In 2020 they started in April, and holiday searches were +80% vs April 2019. — [Pinterest Business insights](https://business.pinterest.com/insights/change-your-plans-your-audience-already-did/); India version: +77%. — [Pinterest India](https://business.pinterest.com/en-in/insights/change-your-plans-your-audience-already-did/)
- [M-PLAT] Festive inspiration searches start as early as July and keep growing through Q4. — [Pinterest festive gifting guide](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/festive-gifting-pinterest/). Halloween searches grow from August. — [Pinterest spooky season](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/scare-up-spooky-seasonal-success/)
- [M-PLAT] About 45% of US Pinners (1 in 2 in other markets) plan to start shopping earlier. Self-gifting wishlists build from October and peak in November, followed by a December surge in gift ideas. These attributions come from search extracts of Pinterest's holiday pages. — [Pinterest Business insights](https://business.pinterest.com/insights/change-your-plans-your-audience-already-did/); [Pinterest festive gifting](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/festive-gifting-pinterest/)
- [CLAIM] Publish seasonal pins about 45 to 90 days before the event. — [Khrizclusive](https://khrizclusive.com/blog/seasonal-pinterest-marketing/); [84Pins](https://84pins.com/pinterest-for-seasonal-businesses/); [Pin Nerds Media](https://pinnerdsmedia.com/pinterest-holiday-strategy/)
- [CLAIM] There is no published Pinterest text-percentage rule, and guides conflict (some say under 20% of the image). Common rejections: too much text, misleading claims, before/after visuals, and a landing page that does not match the ad. — [Cropink](https://cropink.com/pinterest-advertising-guidelines); [MB Advertising](https://www.mbadv.agency/pinterest-ads/designing-pins-for-pinterest-ads)

### Inferences
- **Pin template for a PDF product:** 2:3 at 1000x1500. Headline overlay in the top third (benefit and audience, e.g. "2026 Budget Planner for Couples"). Mockup in the middle (cover plus fanned interior pages, or a tablet). Bottom band with page count, format and a small logo. Variants rotate the background colour and mockup angle.
- **Seasonal calendar for planners and printables:** have creative live 2 to 3 months before demand (for example, New Year planners from October, back-to-school templates from June, holiday printables from July to September). Refresh as each season starts.
- Since Idea Pins are gone, multi-page "flip-through" content on Pinterest becomes a short vertical video Pin or a carousel Pin.
- Before/after visuals are a rejection risk on Pinterest. Use "without / with the template" framing carefully, or skip it there.

### Gaps
- No measured data on text overlays (presence, size, coverage) for Pinterest ads was found.
- No Pinterest data specific to digital products, printables or planners was found.
- A figure that Pinterest Predicts has an "88% six-year accuracy" came from a secondary blog and was not verified on Pinterest's own pages.
- An agency claim that static Pins beat video Pins on CTR (about 17x) was seen in search extracts without a traceable source.

### Inferences
- **Weighting for the report writer:** (1) Meta's and Google's controlled split and lift tests on placement or format choices, such as 9:16 with sound vs images, or creator mixes. (2) Motion's large observational Meta dataset for ranking formats and hooks, discounting promotional items for the Black Friday window. (3) TikTok's 2021 attribute lifts, which are directionally consistent but old and lack base rates. (4) Academic work for engagement mechanics. (5) Vendor blogs only as hypotheses.
- Where sources converge, the rule is robust enough to hard-code: 9:16, sound, early product/brand, on-screen text CTA, several scenes, creator variants, creative volume and diversity. Where they diverge or are thin (hook type, ideal length, refresh interval, face vs no face), make it a test variable rather than a rule.
- A low-ticket digital-product advertiser should measure winners on CPA including order-bump and upsell revenue (see the Roaspy claim in section 1). Motion's spend-based hit rate can reward ads that Meta spends on, not the ones that are profitable.
